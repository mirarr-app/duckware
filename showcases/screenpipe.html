01 · capture

Your day, indexed as it happens

Screenpipe listens for meaningful events — app switches, typing pauses, clicks, scrolls — then pairs a frame with structured OS accessibility text. Audio runs through local Whisper. Idle machines stay quiet; busy ones stay remembered.

CH·01

Screen + a11y

Accessibility tree first; OCR only when the OS can’t speak the UI.

CH·02

Audio + speakers

Mic and system audio transcribed locally, with speaker diarization.

CH·03

Inputs & apps

Keyboard context, window titles, and app switches land on the same timeline.

CH·04

Filters

Drop passwords, windows, and PII before anything hits storage.

02 · local pipeline

Record → vault → agent socket

Everything stays on-device by default: JPEG frames on disk, SQLite FTS5 for search, a localhost API, and markdown “pipes” that schedule agents against your real work.

04 · private by construction

on-device
default path

Local, private, auditable

Screenpipe positions itself as a source-available alternative to Rewind / Limitless and Microsoft Recall — with the important difference that you can inspect the capture path and keep the index offline.

  • 01
    Nothing leaves unless you opt in

    Core capture, search, and timeline stay local. Cloud AI and sync are explicit choices.

  • 02
    Deterministic pipe permissions

    YAML allow/deny rules gate apps, windows, content types, and endpoints — enforced outside the prompt.

  • 03
    You own the vault

    Export, wipe, or encrypt at rest. No account required for the core loop.
