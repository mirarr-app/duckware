01 · Why it matters

Edit without renting the scissors

CapCut made short-form editing feel approachable — then locked more of that simplicity behind accounts and paywalls. OpenCut answers with an MIT-licensed editor that runs where creators already work: the browser first, with desktop and mobile on the same roadmap. Media stays on your machine; the product is the timeline, not the telemetry.

MARK IN · privacy

Local by default

Projects and media live in the browser/device path. You’re assembling cuts, not uploading a rough cut to someone else’s cloud to unlock “basic.”

MARK IN · craft

Real NLE muscle

Classic OpenCut already covers the CapCut-shaped loop: multi-track timeline, captions, masks, speed/volume, stickers, and browser export — without treating those as premium SKUs.

MARK IN · future

Rewrite with teeth

The ground-up rebuild targets one Rust core across web, desktop, and mobile, plus plugins, an Editor API, MCP for agents, and headless batch renders.
